Data Science Methodology

Main steps used by the analyzer.

The system uses historical rainfall data together with user property information to estimate rainwater harvesting potential.

01 · User Input

Location, roof area, roof type, people, daily water requirement and purpose are collected.

02 · Data Cleaning

Invalid dates and invalid rainfall values are removed before analysis.

03 · Location Selection

The rainfall dataset location nearest to the selected city is identified.

04 · Rainfall Analysis

Historical annual and monthly rainfall patterns are calculated.

05 · Rainfall Prediction

A Random Forest regression model estimates rainfall for the prediction year.

06 · Harvesting Calculation

Rainfall, roof area and runoff coefficient are used to estimate harvestable water.

07 · Storage Recommendation

Water availability and monthly requirement are used to recommend a practical tank size.

08 · Feasibility

Harvesting potential is compared with monthly water demand to generate a score.
