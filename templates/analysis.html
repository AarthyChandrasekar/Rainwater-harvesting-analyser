🔬 Data Science Methodology

How the analyzer converts your property and rainfall data into recommendations.

The system combines historical rainfall data, location matching, machine-learning prediction and rooftop harvesting calculations.

01 · User Input

Location, roof area, roof type, people, daily water requirement and purpose are collected.

02 · Data Cleaning

Invalid dates and rainfall values are removed before analysis.

03 · Location Matching

The rainfall dataset location nearest to the selected city is identified.

04 · Rainfall Analysis

Historical annual and monthly rainfall patterns are calculated.

05 · ML Prediction

A Random Forest regression model estimates rainfall for the prediction year.

06 · Harvesting

Rainfall, roof area and runoff coefficient estimate harvestable water.

07 · Storage

Water availability and monthly requirement determine a practical tank size.

08 · Feasibility

Harvesting potential is compared with monthly demand to generate a score.
